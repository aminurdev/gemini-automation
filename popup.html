Bulk Image Generator

For Google Gemini

v1.0

🚀 Quick Start

  1. Open gemini.google.com in your browser.
  2. Look for the floating ✨ Bulk Prompt widget in the top-right corner.
  3. Paste your prompts (one per line) and click Start Generation.
  4. Gemini will generate images one by one automatically!
